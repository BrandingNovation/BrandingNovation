### Technical Risks
- **Risk**: Platform downtime during peak usage
- **Mitigation**: Auto-scaling infrastructure, load testing
- **Backup**: 99.9% uptime SLA with credits for downtime

### Market Risks
- **Risk**: Competition from established players (Zapier, Microsoft)
- **Mitigation**: Focus on AI differentiation and superior UX
- **Backup**: Rapid feature development and customer lock-in

### Financial Risks
- **Risk**: Higher customer acquisition costs than projected
- **Mitigation**: Diversified marketing channels, referral programs
- **Backup**: Extend runway with pre-seed funding if needed

### Legal Risks
- **Risk**: Data privacy regulations and compliance
- **Mitigation**: GDPR compliance, security audits, insurance
- **Backup**: Legal counsel on retainer for quick response
